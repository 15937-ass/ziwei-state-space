Tracking Issue: #588  
Lane: PRODUCT_ONLY  
Status: IMPLEMENTED / EXACT-HEAD VALIDATION PENDING

## 1. Purpose

This artifact extends the Wang 24-case corpus from target-palace-only blind evidence to reproducible, source-input-reconstructed full natal-chart snapshots for the 11 cases that passed the complete source-bounded candidate probe with exactly one independent target-anchor match.

The materialized chart is generated by the pinned Zhongzhou chart engine. It is **not** a claim that every star, palace annotation, transformation, or other generated field was independently transcribed from the printed source chart.

## 2. Eligible case set

Exactly these 11 cases may be materialized:

`WANG-CASE-001, 003, 006, 009, 010, 012, 013, 015, 019, 021, 023`

The other 13 cases remain excluded from this artifact:
- six `MULTIPLE_ANCHOR_MATCH`;
- five `NO_ANCHOR_MATCH`;
- two `BLOCKED_SOURCE_INCOMPLETE`;
- zero `ENGINE_UNSUPPORTED` in the accepted v0.1 readiness census.

No excluded case is silently repaired, guessed, or promoted.

## 3. Reconstruction contract

- Engine: `iztro-js@2.6.1`
- Entry point: `astro.byLunar`
- Algorithm: `zhongzhou`
- Input calendar: lunar
- `astro_type=heaven`
- `fix_leap=true`
- `day_divide=forward`
- `year_divide=normal`
- `horoscope_divide=normal`

The materializer re-evaluates every source-bounded candidate for each eligible case and selects a chart only when exactly one candidate matches the accepted independent target anchor and its candidate ID agrees with Product #545's readiness artifact.

For WANG-CASE-021, the source's unqualified 子时 alternatives remain explicit. The materializer records both evaluated candidate IDs and the single anchor-matching selection; it does not rewrite the source signature as if it had originally specified one time.

## 4. What is verified

For each materialized chart, the independent anchor check covers only:
- target-palace identity within the approved Product palace-alias map;
- direct fourteen-major-star system when the target palace has main stars;
- source-visible opposite-palace borrowed major-star system when the target palace is empty;
- consistency of the target-palace empty-main-star state.

The artifact preserves:
- source chart span and page references;
- source-input fields used by the engine;
- all source-bounded candidate IDs evaluated;
- the unique matched candidate ID;
- per-candidate anchor-match status;
- the full normalized engine-generated chart snapshot;
- a SHA-256 digest of each normalized chart snapshot.

## 5. What is not verified

The target-palace anchor does not establish full-source-chart equivalence. Non-target palace content, auxiliary stars, transformations, stems/branches, age bands, and other chart output remain **engine-generated fields**, not independently source-verified source facts.

This artifact does not establish:
- expert-method fidelity;
- predictive validity;
- empirical manifestation probabilities;
- canonical Zhongzhou Knowledge;
- broader MethodPattern target scopes.

The historical `BLIND_EVIDENCE_V0_1.json` remains immutable and continues to describe its original target-palace-only scope.

## 6. Blindness and semantic boundaries

The artifact must not contain subject labels, person-group IDs, expert reasoning graphs, expert conclusions, outcome references, expert prose, or biography. It must not backfill missing source input, change canonical Knowledge, add probabilities/weights/thresholds, or claim predictive validity.
