# Modeling Assumptions

Version: 0.1

## A1 — Latent-state assumption
Repeated textual properties can be represented by a finite or hierarchically finite set of latent variables.

## A2 — Compositional but not necessarily linear
Star systems may contain emergent interaction terms beyond component-star properties.

## A3 — Context dependence
The same latent state can project differently in different palaces and temporal contexts.

## A4 — State-dependent temporal response
A given temporal configuration need not produce the same response for different natal states.

## A5 — Mixed representation
Not all latent properties must be represented by ordinary signed Euclidean axes. Relational structures such as the five elements may require graph/matrix components.

## A6 — Symbolic parameters before empirical fitting
Unknown gains remain symbolic until later case-based system identification.

## A7 — External inputs are not control inputs
Decade/year/month astrological layers are modeled as exogenous temporal inputs. Human decisions, if modeled later, form a separate control-input class.

## A8 — Internal fidelity is not predictive validation
Successful reconstruction of Zhongzhou interpretations does not establish real-world predictive power.

## A9 — Contradictions are data
Conflicting textual rules are recorded and analyzed, not silently harmonized.

## A10 — Ontology is revisable
The candidate qi ontology may be merged, split, or rejected based on textual redundancy, structural observability, and later empirical identifiability.
