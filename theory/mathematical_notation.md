## Constraint notation

- `alpha > 0` — positive directional effect
- `alpha < 0` — negative directional effect
- `alpha_A > alpha_B` — ordinal comparison
- `alpha = ?` — unknown, to be estimated later

Numeric equality is forbidden before empirical identification unless explicitly illustrative.
