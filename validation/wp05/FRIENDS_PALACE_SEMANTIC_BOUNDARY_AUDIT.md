# WP05 P08 Friends/Subordinates Palace Semantic Boundary Audit

Status: **SOURCE-VERIFIED — EXISTING ONTOLOGY CONFIRMED**

## Source scope
Canonical source: SRC-ZZ-003, Friends/Subordinates Palace opening, printed p122.

## Source-grounded boundary
The source explicitly rejects treating 交友宫 as a generic "all friends" palace.

- Primary P08 scene: employees, ordinary subordinates, lower-generation or lower-status contacts.
- P02 兄弟宫 remains primary for same-generation friends and peers. P08 can modify the breadth and quality of that peer network because it opposes P02, but it does not replace P02.
- P04 子女宫 receives people personally cultivated, promoted or supported by the native, including 门生弟子 and comparable protégés.
- P08 relationship judgment must also compare the strength of P01 命宫: P08 too strong versus weak P01 may produce subordinate dominance; P01 too strong versus weak P08 may produce little subordinate support and more personal burden.
- The source's preferred state is balance rather than maximizing P08 strength.

## Ontology decision
No new palace primitive is required. Existing P02/P04/P08 boundary notes already encode the same distinction. This audit upgrades that existing boundary from design-consistent to directly source-verified for the P08 chapter opening.

## Human Gate
None. No new temporal mechanism, memory kind, weight, gain, probability or decay term is introduced.
