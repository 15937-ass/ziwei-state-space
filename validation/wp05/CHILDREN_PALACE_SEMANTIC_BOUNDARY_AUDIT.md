# WP05 Children Palace Semantic Boundary Audit

Status: **SOURCE-VERIFIED — EXISTING ONTOLOGY CONFIRMED**

The Children Palace chapter explicitly supports two semantic scenes:

1. biological/parent-child scene: child relationship, child personality and the traditional child-count layer;
2. cultivated-junior scene: disciples, protégés, personally promoted juniors, trusted assistants and right-hand aides.

Boundary against Friends Palace is explicit in the source example:
- ordinary shop staff / ordinary subordinate relation -> Friends Palace;
- a staff member who becomes the native's right hand and is intentionally cultivated to handle business independently -> Children Palace.

Implementation consequence:
- preserve P04 children / child_relationship and younger_generation / proteges / trusted_subordinates;
- preserve P08 for ordinary subordinate/network relations;
- do not collapse all subordinates into P08 and do not expand P04 to generic friendship;
- traditional child-number formulas are not promoted into modern latent-state primitives.
