# WP05 P11 Wellbeing/Mental Enjoyment Palace Semantic Boundary Audit

Status: **BOUNDARY_DEFINED — SOURCE GROUNDED**

## Scope
- palace: P11 福德宫
- canonical source: SRC-ZZ-003
- opening source span: printed pp166–167
- ontology baseline: mental_enjoyment; thought_activity; psychological_state
- secondary scope: habits; interests; subjective_wellbeing; cognitive_style

## Source-defined semantic scope
The source explicitly distinguishes P11 from P01 命宫:
1. P11 primarily evaluates mental enjoyment and thought activity;
2. P01 is more oriented toward material enjoyment / lived expression;
3. material comfort and mental enjoyment can diverge;
4. subjective happiness therefore cannot be inferred from wealth or material condition alone.

P11 is also explicitly used for:
- interests and special hobbies;
- cultural/artistic interests;
- sensual or addictive habits;
- anxiety, agitation and inner unrest;
- cognitive style, including concentration, rumination, rigidity and unusual thought patterns;
- broad mental-health or abnormal-psychology candidates in severe source-specific configurations.

## Hard boundaries

### P11 is not a generic psychiatric-diagnosis engine
The source contains highly concrete labels such as intellectual disability, autism-like states, abnormal psychology, compulsive or deviant habits and self-destructive tendencies.

These must remain:
- EVENT_CANDIDATE / CASE_SUPPORT_ONLY when concrete or clinical;
- source-specific evidence rather than deterministic diagnosis;
- dependent on the source's explicit combination and cross-palace context.

No clinical diagnosis is inferred from a single P11 rule.

### Mental enjoyment is not equivalent to wealth
The source explicitly says:
- poor wealth can coexist with strong mental enjoyment;
- wealth can coexist with emptiness or distress;
- P05 财帛 and P11 therefore interact but must remain separate targets.

### P11 is not a substitute for P01
The source permits some P11 mental/cognitive properties to help interpret P01, but does not collapse the two palaces.

P11 may refine:
- subjective wellbeing;
- cognitive style;
- inner motivation;
- psychological response.

It must not overwrite general P01 identity/state semantics.

### Three-party context is structural
The source explicitly notes that an unfavorable P11 with favorable trines can produce specific compensating forms:
- P03 夫妻 favorable -> enjoyment may rely on spouse/partner context;
- P05 财帛 favorable -> enjoyment may require expenditure/material support;
- P07 迁移 favorable -> leaving the birth/home environment may improve subjective wellbeing.

These are cross-palace constraints, not exceptions to the P11 boundary.

### Hobby / desire / compulsion must be separated
The source spans a continuum from:
- ordinary interests;
- artistic or intellectual hobbies;
- sensual enjoyment;
- material desire;
- compulsive/addictive or harmful habits.

These must not be collapsed into one generic “desire” variable.

## Cross-palace constraints
P11 commonly requires context from:
- P01 命宫: identity, general functioning and material/lived expression;
- P03 夫妻宫: partner-mediated enjoyment or distress;
- P05 财帛宫: material resources and the cost/means of enjoyment;
- P07 迁移宫: environmental relocation and subjective wellbeing away from birthplace;
- P06 疾厄宫: when the source explicitly links mental enjoyment to physical illness or impairment.

## Temporal / history policy
The opening source is predominantly structural and state-descriptive.

Ordered relations such as:
- repeated rigidity -> later frustration;
- material pursuit -> persistent mental emptiness;
- favorable/unfavorable environmental relocation -> later subjective relief/distress;
may use existing PATH_DEPENDENT_GATE only when the ordering is explicit in the source.

No new memory type is pre-authorized.

## Epistemic policy
Clinical-sounding historical language is retained only as source evidence and must be normalized cautiously.
The system must not modernize a source term into a stronger psychiatric diagnosis than the text itself supports.
