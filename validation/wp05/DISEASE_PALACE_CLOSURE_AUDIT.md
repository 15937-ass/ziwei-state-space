## Semantic closure
P06 is not modeled as a deterministic disease-name lookup. The source supports a layered structure:

**natal constitution / susceptibility**
→ **body-system and five-element mechanism**
→ **cross-palace context**
→ **decade/year activation**
→ **possible symptom or clinical-event candidate**

Reusable inference is therefore restricted to:
- constitution and susceptibility axes;
- source-defined five-element/body-system mechanisms;
- cross-palace requirements;
- qualitative modifiers and severity constraints;
- temporal activation structure;
- source-explicit differentiation and uncertainty rules.

Concrete diagnoses, surgery, disability, cancer, infectious disease, reproductive pathology, poisoning and other named outcomes remain EVENT_CANDIDATE + CASE_SUPPORT_ONLY.

The chapter's later disease-name-first sections are treated as a secondary reindex of the same theory, not as an independent second rule system.
