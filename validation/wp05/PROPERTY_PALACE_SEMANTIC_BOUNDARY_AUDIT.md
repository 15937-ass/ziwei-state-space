# WP05 P10 Property/Home Palace Semantic Boundary Audit

Status: **BOUNDARY_DEFINED — SOURCE GROUNDED**

## Scope
- palace: P10 田宅宫
- canonical source: SRC-ZZ-003
- opening source span: printed p156
- ontology baseline: property; home; real_estate
- secondary scope: household; work_environment; service_institution

## Source-defined semantic scope
The source explicitly gives P10 several distinct responsibilities:
1. household/home rise and decline;
2. property/estate gain, loss and preservation;
3. elder mourning / family bereavement signals;
4. rise, decline and personnel environment of the organization in which the native works;
5. change, relocation and reorganization of residence or company/workplace;
6. the native's own company can also be treated as a service institution when the native personally works in it.

These are not collapsed into a single “property luck” variable.

## Hard boundaries

### P10 is not a generic investment-profit palace
The source explicitly warns that modern buying/selling of property can be speculative, and the speculative-profit outcome is generally **not** determined by P10 alone.

Therefore:
- property ownership / acquisition / preservation / relocation can belong to P10;
- investment return, speculation quality and capital gain require P05 财帛 and other relevant context;
- business success of a real-estate company is not automatically a P10 result.

### Property events can be cross-palace
The source gives historical examples in which household/property benefit or loss is expressed through other family palaces because the causal event belongs to a relative.

Therefore:
- absence of a P10 indicator does not prove absence of a property event;
- P04 children, P02 siblings and other family palaces may contain the causal source;
- P10 should not absorb every family-caused property outcome.

### Service institution is a distinct projection
Employer/company rise and decline, organizational relocation and workplace environment are valid P10 projections.

This is distinct from:
- P09 career role / industry nature;
- P05 personal financial flow;
- P01 personal state.

The same organization may therefore project into multiple palaces with different target semantics.

### Bereavement is event-layer material
The source permits elder-mourning interpretation from P10 and notes a stronger historical applicability for the eldest-son/eldest-grandson context.

Concrete death/bereavement statements are therefore:
- EVENT_CANDIDATE or CASE_SUPPORT_ONLY when highly specific;
- not reusable as a generic latent “bad property” primitive;
- subject to time-layer and family-context checks.

### Residence / workplace movement must remain separate from ownership
P10 movement includes:
- house relocation;
- company relocation;
- workplace change;
- internal office/room change;
- renovation in some configurations.

Movement does not automatically imply buying/selling property.

## Cross-palace constraints
P10 commonly requires context from:
- P01 命宫: whether the native can use or sustain the property/institution pattern;
- P02 兄弟宫: sibling-caused property loss or division;
- P04 子女宫: child-caused household/property benefit;
- P05 财帛宫: capital, investment and financial return;
- P06 疾厄宫: the P10 triad relation and some environmental/body interactions;
- P09 事业宫: distinction between service-institution condition and the native's career role;
- P12 父母宫 where family/elder context is required.

## Temporal / history policy
Ordered source statements such as:
- inherited property -> decline -> self-acquisition;
- sale -> later repurchase;
- relocation -> later stabilization;
- sudden gain -> sudden loss;
may use the existing PATH_DEPENDENT_GATE when the sequence is explicit.

No new memory type is pre-authorized.

## Scene translation
Historical labels for ancestral land, large compounds, farms, mines, old houses, public property, family business and service institutions are preserved as source scenes and translated cautiously.

No ancient property form is treated as a mandatory modern one-to-one object class.

## Closure of boundary audit
P10 can now enter source-span extraction.

No new primitive, memory type, Human Gate, independent time scale, numeric weight, probability or decay is introduced by the boundary definition.
