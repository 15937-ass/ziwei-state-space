# WP-02 Ontology Red-Team Review — v0.1 Proposal

Status: PRE-G2  
Input:
- `knowledge/pilot_rules_100_v0.2.csv`

## 1. Overall assessment

The proposal is directionally sound and respects the project constitution:
- no arbitrary continuous weights,
- primitive-state candidates are separated from obvious outcome variables,
- five elements are kept relational rather than collapsed to one axis,
- uncertainty is explicit.

However, the current ontology is **not ready to freeze as the complete qi basis** because it is derived from only four of the fourteen major stars.

## 2. Candidates likely to survive expansion

### High-confidence
- 发射—收敛
- 刚烈—柔和
- 沉实—空泛
- 认知机变

These have either repeated direct source support or cross-star support.

### Medium-confidence
- 稳定—变动
- 审慎—躁决
- 行动驱动
- 关系整合—孤离

These are plausible but may split or merge after the remaining ten stars are extracted.

## 3. Candidates at risk of being high-level composites

### 主导—协调
“领导力/独断” may be generated by:
- action drive,
- relational structure,
- authority/status context,
rather than being a primitive qi coordinate.

Recommendation: keep low-confidence and outside the frozen core.

### 竞争激活
Likely a relation/environment state rather than a primitive intrinsic qi variable.

Recommendation: move to a relational-dynamics layer unless later texts repeatedly treat it as intrinsic.

## 4. Potential axis-design problem

`审慎—躁决` may not be a true bipolar line.

Evidence includes:
- 计划延缓,
- 优柔寡断,
- 躁决,
- 冲动.

This suggests at least three regimes:
1. under-activation / indecision,
2. calibrated deliberation,
3. over-activation / impulsivity.

Recommendation:
- represent as a scalar with an optimum region, or
- separate “decision latency” from “impulsivity”.

Do not freeze it as a simple signed axis yet.

## 5. Groundedness axis requires split test

`沉实—空泛` currently merges:
- 质性/具体,
- 行动落地,
- 空虚,
- 空想.

Possible decomposition:
- concreteness ↔ abstraction,
- realization/implementation capacity.

Recommendation: keep one candidate for v0.1, but mark mandatory split-test after the remaining stars.

## 6. Relational integration may contain two distinct variables

Current evidence combines:
- internal孤克/疏离,
- external助力/属下资源.

A person can theoretically be internally solitary while still possessing a large support network.

Recommendation: test split:
- relational disposition / isolation,
- network support / available allies.

## 7. Five elements and yin-yang

Current treatment is appropriately conservative.

Do not yet force:
- 阴 = -1,
- 阳 = +1,
or assign element mixture weights.

The first formal representation should preserve:
- star-declared yin/yang metadata,
- star-declared element identity,
- directed 生/克 relations as a separate relation layer.

Numeric embedding can be postponed.

## 8. Critical methodological finding

The ontology should not be built only by clustering words.

It should be built by asking whether a candidate variable improves compression and explanatory reuse across rules.

A useful candidate should:
1. explain multiple rules,
2. remain semantically stable across stars,
3. avoid direct dependence on one palace/outcome,
4. support explicit operators (increase/decrease/transform),
5. not be reducible to another approved variable.

This suggests an ontology-selection criterion for later versions.
