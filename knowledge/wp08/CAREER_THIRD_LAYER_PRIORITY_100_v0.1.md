## Why most of the 90 remain blocked
Relation words such as `同度 / 同宫 / 同会 / 见 / 会 / 朝拱` tell us where/how a group is related, but usually do not tell us whether a shorthand such as `昌曲 / 空劫 / 魁钺 / 辅弼 / 火铃 / 羊陀` requires one member, both members, or another source-local realization. Closed membership is therefore preserved separately from activation.

Likewise, `煞忌 / 刑耗 / 煞刑 / 煞耗` remain source compounds unless the source itself supplies internal boolean composition. Plain `吉 / 凶` is retained as whole-configuration valence rather than converted into a star list.
