## What the 100 rules add

### 1. Tianji: external earning is not effortless earning
- **天机太阴**: going out to seek wealth is favorable in the source, but still accompanied by constant busyness. Lu strengthens the reading; 煞忌刑耗 can reverse it toward loss. Mixed auspicious/adverse factors can mean gain with disputes or success with setbacks.
- **天机巨门**: the source emphasizes mobility and outward business, especially sales, communication and travel-related work; adverse combinations bring failure or pressure.
- **天机天梁 vs 天机巨门**: the source explicitly contrasts attachment to the homeland (机梁) with weaker attachment / outward movement (机巨). Do not collapse them.

### 2. Solar: movement, visibility and status are not the same as wealth
Solar in the Migration Palace is framed as active/expansive, favorable to leaving the birthplace, study, further training, credentials, visibility and social standing. The source explicitly says Solar Hua Lu alone does not establish wealth from migration. The ancient female remarriage reading remains bounded by its historical social context and is not generalized to modern people.

### 3. Wuqu: the actual system and modifier location decide the direction
Wu-Tan, Wu-Fu, Wu-Xiang, Wu-Sha, Wu-Po and single Wuqu opposite Tanlang remain distinct. The source gives conditional contrasts among patronage, pressure, stability, wandering, setback and wealth. In particular, the same 火铃夹 motif changes sign according to whether it flanks Wuqu itself or the opposite Tanlang; this is a source-bounded system-position dependency, not a universal Fire/Bell rule.

### 4. Tiantong: 纳福 is not automatic wealth
The source distinguishes ease/stability/help from wealth. 天同化禄 alone is not “outbound wealth”; the wealth reading requires 禄存叠冲. Malefic modifiers, travel loss, theft and illness clauses remain source-conditioned, and concrete severe events stay CASE_SUPPORT_ONLY.
